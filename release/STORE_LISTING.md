## Full description
Spell Pals helps children aged 4 to 9 learn to spell and read with Leo the lion and Bella the bunny.

**Made for your child's age and level**
Tell Spell Pals your child's name and age and it chooses the right words. There are five levels, from short 3-letter words
for age 4 to long words like "elephant" and "strawberry" for ages 8 and 9. The app notices which words are tricky and brings
them back, and moves up a level when your child is ready.

**Seven games**
• Spell the Word — tap the letters in order
• Pick the Letter — find the first or last sound
• Sound Out — build a word from its sounds
• Match the Picture — read the word, find the picture
• Fill the Vowel — find the missing middle sound
• Rhyme Time — find the word that sounds the same
• Memory Match — flip the cards and find the pairs

**30 picture stories**
Stories for every level, including personal stories that star your child and their buddy. Press "Read to me" and each word
lights up as it is read, tap any word to hear it, then answer fun questions to earn stars.

**An adventure map**
Three worlds, fifteen stages and three boss stages. Every stage opens the next one.

**Kind and encouraging**
Happy sounds and a friendly voice cheer every right answer. Wrong answers get a gentle "Try again!" and a hint, never a
penalty. There are no timers.

**Safe for children**
• No ads
• No accounts or sign-in
• No tracking, and no data leaves the device
• A Grown-ups area, protected by a PIN, with a weekly summary, Star Words and settings
