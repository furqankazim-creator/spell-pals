# Privacy Policy for Spell Pals

_Last updated: [DATE]_

**Spell Pals** is a spelling and reading app for children aged 4 to 9, published by **[YOUR NAME OR COMPANY]** ("we").
We designed it to collect nothing about your child.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Spell Pals does not collect, send, sell or share any personal information.

- There are **no accounts** and no sign-in.
- There are **no ads** and no third-party advertising or analytics tools.
- There is **no tracking** of any kind.
- The app does **not** use the camera, the microphone, the contact list or the location.

## What stays on the device

When a parent or child sets up the app, it stores the following **only on that device**:

- the child's first name (or nickname), age and chosen buddy picture;
- play progress: levels, stars, stickers, words practised, stories read, adventure-map progress and daily play time;
- the parent settings (sound on or off, daily goal, the grown-ups PIN).

This information never leaves the device. A parent can erase it at any time with **Grown-ups → Settings → Reset this child**,
or by uninstalling the app.

## Spoken voice

The app speaks words and encouragement using the **text-to-speech voice built into the phone or tablet**. Depending on the
device manufacturer, that voice may be processed by the device's own speech service. Spell Pals does not send any text to
our own servers.

## Children's privacy

Because we collect no personal information, we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (US COPPA) or
under 16 (EU GDPR-K). The Grown-ups area is protected by a PIN so that settings and the reset button are for adults.

## Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the app works in a way that affects privacy, we will update this page and the app version notes
before the change takes effect.
